How it works

From idea to finished build

Describe it

Say what you want to build and which parts you already have.

“A traffic light for an Arduino Uno, with a crosswalk button.”

We design it

An AI builds it from measured parts, then checks the fit, the power and every pin.

You build it

Follow one step at a time. Your camera checks each one.

The guides

One step. One part. No guessing.

Parts slide into place and wires draw themselves, so you see exactly what to do.

  • Exact holes. Row 10, column e. Not “near the middle”.
  • The why. Every step says what the part is for.
  • Test checkpoints. Catch a wiring mistake before it spreads.
  • A parts list with sizes and links to buy.
